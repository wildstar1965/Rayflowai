RayFlow original synthesized click sounds

soft.wav, wood.wav and tick.wav are synthesized from mathematical sine waves
and deterministic noise for this project. No third-party recordings are used.

To the extent possible under law, the authors dedicate these audio assets to
the public domain under CC0 1.0 Universal:
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

48 kHz mono PCM16; 100 ms; peak amplitude -6 dBFS before project volume.
